About Me

Background

I am currently a Visiting Research Scholar at Michigan State University. I recently completed my Master's degree here in Computer Science, where I completed my thesis under the supervision of Dr. Wolfgang Kerzendorf in the department of Physics and Astronomy. My research is computational, specifically focusing on enhancing convergence in supernova simulations using numerical optimization and high-performance computing techniques.

Research Focus

My thesis titled "Towards Faster Supernovae Simulations: Enhancing Convergence in TARDIS with Adaptive Damping" explores innovative methods to accelerate Monte Carlo radiative transfer simulations. I have developed adaptive damping strategies that have been successfully integrated into the TARDIS production codebase, ready for use for faster convergence across all supernova simulations.

Professional Experience

Prior to my graduate studies, I worked as a Senior Analyst in Cybersecurity at PricewaterhouseCoopers India, where I developed anomaly detection pipelines and led cybersecurity audits aligned with RBI regulations.
